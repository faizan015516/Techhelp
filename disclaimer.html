Independence and Safety Disclaimer

Printer Resolve is an independent information website. We are not affiliated with, authorized by, sponsored by or endorsed by HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, Microsoft or any other printer, software or computer manufacturer.

Brand names and official resources

All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify products discussed in our guides. For manufacturer warranty, replacement parts, firmware and official driver downloads, visit the manufacturer's own website.

Illustrative printer images and copyright

The brand-labeled printer images on this website are original concept illustrations or interactive 3D models, not official product photographs or exact representations of any manufacturer model. Brand names appear as descriptive identifiers, not copied manufacturer logos. We do not claim manufacturer sponsorship, and model-specific physical features should be verified using the maker's official documentation. If third-party images are used in future, they should only be published with appropriate usage rights and any required attribution.

Safety and limitations

Our tutorials provide general troubleshooting information, not a guarantee of repair. Instructions vary by model and configuration. Make a backup where appropriate, and stop if instructions could cause damage or a safety issue. Electrical, mechanical and hardware repairs may require qualified in-person assistance.

Transparency is part of the experience.

Illustrations explain common problems; they are not photographs of devices we have tested. Readers should consult official model guidance for specific procedures.

Protect yourself from support scams

We do not require remote access, payment, account passwords, banking details or one-time verification codes to read our articles. Treat unsolicited pop-ups, calls and demands for remote access with caution. See our remote-access safety guide.

Report a safety or copyright concern

If a guide appears unsafe, a printer-model instruction is incorrect, or an illustration raises a rights concern, email support@printerresolve.com with the public page URL and a short description. Do not send passwords, private configuration reports, serial numbers or other confidential information. See our editorial correction standards.
