About Printer Resolve

Printer Resolve is an independent technology help website and remote support service. We publish free, step-by-step troubleshooting guides for printer setup, connectivity, Windows 11 PCs and everyday device problems. Visitors can use the guides without purchasing support.

What we help with

Our guides explain common printer offline messages, print queues, Wi-Fi setup, scanning, Windows settings and how to find official drivers. We focus on problems that home users and small businesses can investigate safely, starting with reversible steps.

Popular starting points include our Windows 11 printer offline guide, printer detection checklist, printer driver troubleshooting guide and remote-assistance safety guide.

How we prepare troubleshooting guides

We aim to explain what an error means, help readers identify likely causes and prioritize lower-risk checks before advanced changes. Where useful, our guides link to official Microsoft or printer-manufacturer instructions so readers can check the steps for their exact device.

Guide instructions may differ across printer models, router configurations and Windows updates. We do not claim to have physically tested every printer or guarantee that a particular fix will work. Manufacturer documentation and device-specific error messages take precedence.

How optional support works

You can use our free articles independently or browse our troubleshooting guides with your device model and a description of the problem. Troubleshooting may begin with written guidance. Any proposed paid service, price and remote-access arrangement should be explained and agreed upon beforehand.

Remote access is not required just to ask for help. Please do not submit account passwords, one-time verification codes, payment credentials or sensitive documents.

Where we operate

Printer Resolve operates from Kashmir, India and offers remote support for enquiries from abroad, including the United States, subject to availability and the suitability of remote troubleshooting. We do not have a physical office in the United States.

Independent service, not a manufacturer

Printer Resolve is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorized by HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, Microsoft or other product manufacturers. Brand names are used solely to identify the devices discussed. Some customers may already qualify for support through their manufacturer or warranty; check those options first.

You can review our independent service disclaimer, terms and cancellation and refund policy before arranging any work.
