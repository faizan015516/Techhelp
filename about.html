About Us

Printer Resolve is an independent remote technology support service for printer setup, connectivity, Windows PCs and everyday device problems.

What we help with

Our support covers common printer offline issues, print queues, Wi-Fi connections, scanning, setup and guidance to official driver downloads. We assist home users and small businesses.

How support works

Describe your issue through the support form. Troubleshooting can begin with guided instructions. Remote access should only begin after you understand and approve the connection.

Where we operate

Printer Resolve operates from Kashmir, India and provides remote technology support. It does not have a physical office in the United States.
