About Printer Resolve

Printer Resolve is an independent information website publishing free, step-by-step troubleshooting guides for common printer, Wi-Fi and Windows 11 problems. You can read our articles without registering, paying or granting remote access.

What you can learn

Our articles cover printers showing offline, stuck print queues, wireless printer setup, official driver installation, blank or faded pages, and common Windows network issues. Start with our printer offline guide, printer not found checklist, or driver unavailable guide.

How our guides are prepared

We aim to describe the symptoms, explain plausible causes and present lower-risk checks before advanced steps. We encourage readers to use official device documentation and manufacturer download pages. Settings and steps may vary by model and Windows version. We do not claim to have physically tested every device.

What we do not offer on this website

Printer Resolve does not currently sell troubleshooting sessions, subscriptions, or remote-control services through this website. We do not ask readers to install remote-access tools, disclose passwords or share payment details to read the guides.

Where we are based

The site is published from Kashmir, India. We do not represent ourselves as a printer manufacturer's official customer-service center or as having a United States office.
