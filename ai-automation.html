Frequently asked

The details matter.

Clear expectations make for a better implementation. Here are a few common questions businesses ask.

Ask about your project ↗
Is the sample preview connected to a real clinic?

No. The interactive preview is a prewritten example. A production system needs approved knowledge, configured integrations, testing and the client's authorization.

Can an assistant confirm appointments automatically?

Only if a suitable scheduling integration is built, authorized and tested. Without that, the assistant should collect an appointment request for reception staff to confirm.

Can patients interact in Arabic?

Arabic and English text flows can be explored. The client or an appropriate reviewer should validate Arabic accuracy and terminology before a production rollout. Business meetings are conducted in English.

What about WhatsApp integration?

We can explore approved WhatsApp Business platform integrations. Eligibility, provider pricing and message-template rules vary. A website demo alone does not mean WhatsApp is already connected.

How is pricing decided?

We first agree on the required features, implementation scope and ongoing support. Setup fees, third-party costs, milestones and payment terms are specified in a written proposal.

What happens with cancellations or refunds?

A project-specific written agreement sets out the scope, milestones, cancellation terms and refund conditions before payment. You can also review our website terms or email us for the applicable service agreement.
