For clinics and service businesses

Support your front desk, not replace it.

For dental and medical practices, an AI assistant should handle administrative inquiries, not provide diagnosis, prescriptions or medical advice. Patient-sensitive data requires appropriate safeguards, permissions and review before any production deployment.

Important demonstration boundary: A prototype may show possible conversation flows, but it is not automatically connected to a clinic's scheduling system, WhatsApp number or electronic medical records. Those integrations require separate technical and privacy assessment.

Common questions

Before starting a project

Is the assistant already integrated with my software?

No. Integration depends on your existing tools, API access, vendor permissions, security requirements and the agreed project scope.

Can an assistant confirm a patient's appointment?

Only after an appropriately authorised booking integration is implemented and tested. Otherwise it should collect a request for staff confirmation.

Can this work in Arabic?

Arabic and English text flows can be configured and demonstrated. Production wording, consistency and customer experience need review by the client or a qualified language reviewer.

How do payments and cancellations work?

We first agree on a written proposal or service agreement covering scope, milestones, payment terms, cancellation and refund conditions. No payments are collected directly on this page. Please request the relevant terms before commissioning work.
