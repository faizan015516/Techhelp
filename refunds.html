Payments and Refunds

Printer Resolve currently publishes free educational troubleshooting guides. There is no paid checkout, subscription enrollment, or remote-support booking on this website, and you do not need to pay to read our articles.

No website purchases

Because no products or services are sold through this website at present, there is ordinarily no website purchase to cancel or refund.

Questions about a past payment

If you believe a payment was previously made to Printer Resolve or you have an unresolved earlier service arrangement, email support@printerresolve.com with the date and a non-sensitive transaction reference. We will review your request and any applicable agreement or consumer rights. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or banking credentials.

Manufacturer fees and third-party purchases

Printer manufacturers, software vendors and other third parties set their own prices and refund policies. Contact the organization that processed your purchase about its charges or warranty coverage.
