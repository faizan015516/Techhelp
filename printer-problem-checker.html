Printer Problem Checker

Not sure which guide to read? Choose the problem you see and how your printer connects. This checker gives you a short sequence of safe tests and links to fuller instructions. It does not inspect your printer, run remote diagnostics or guarantee a repair.

Before starting: Check the printer screen for a warning, note the exact model, and keep track of what you change. Stop if the device has a burning smell, damaged power cable or another electrical hazard. For shared or managed office printers, speak to your administrator before changing settings.

Your troubleshooting checklist

    How to interpret what you find

    Your observationWhat to investigate next
    The printer cannot print its own built-in test/report pageCheck the printer display, paper, consumables and model manual before changing Windows.
    Built-in page works but Windows cannot see the printerInvestigate USB or network discovery, not print quality.
    Windows sees the printer but a specific app failsPrint from another app to isolate an application or document problem.
    A single job fails while other documents printCheck file complexity, queue status and that application's print settings before reinstalling drivers.

    What this checker cannot do

    This is a local, educational checklist for common printer problems. Your selections are processed in your browser; this tool does not submit them to Printer Resolve. It cannot verify your exact printer model, check hardware, inspect network security or replace official manufacturer instructions. Menu names differ by model and operating system. For warranty or physical repair, use the manufacturer's official resources.

    Keep a private troubleshooting record

    Record what you observed. Your entries stay in this browser until you copy them. Never include passwords, serial numbers or personal information.
