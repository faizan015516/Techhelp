Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent information website Printer Resolve, published from Kashmir, India, handles information. For questions or privacy requests, email support@printerresolve.com.

Information you choose to send

The site has no support request form, account registration or paid checkout. If you contact us by email, we receive the address, message and attachments you send. We use these to respond to website enquiries, review editorial corrections, and handle privacy or legal requests. We do not sell email correspondence.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, full card numbers or bank details. This site does not need these to provide free guides.

Hosting and email providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages and uses Zoho for business email. These services may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, security events and message-delivery information. See the Cloudflare privacy policy and Zoho privacy policy.

Advertising, Google's cookies and your choices

Printer Resolve currently includes Google AdSense application code. Its presence does not mean the website has been approved for ads or that advertisements are currently displayed. Because this JavaScript can load from a Google domain, the browser may send technical request information to Google even during an AdSense application or site review; the absence of a visible advertisement does not necessarily mean no third-party network request occurred.

If Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. As part of ad serving, third parties may place or read cookies in a visitor’s browser or use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to collect information. These technologies may also measure ad delivery and performance.

You can opt out of Google personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can read more about Google's use of data on partner websites and advertising technologies. Your browser may also let you block or clear cookies, although that can affect some services. These links control Google's advertising preferences; they are not a claim that this website has its own operational cookie-settings panel.

If third-party ad vendors or networks other than Google are permitted to serve ads through our selected ad settings, we will identify the applicable providers and provide links and available opt-out options before ad delivery. Google's ad program can involve multiple vendors; this policy does not itself constitute an active consent interface or an exhaustive vendor list. Our disclosures must stay aligned with the vendors and consent choices actually configured.

Advertising consent and privacy preferences

Where required, advertising choices must be collected through a working consent-management mechanism, not merely described in this policy. Any consent message or privacy-settings controls are configured through the advertising consent provider. If the message is available to you, use its own controls to consent, decline or manage preferences. If no consent option appears when you expect one, please contact support@printerresolve.com before sharing personal information. Google Ads Settings is a separate Google service and does not replace website-level consent choices.

Regional consent for advertising

Before enabling personalized advertising for visitors in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom or Switzerland, we must meet Google's regional consent requirements, including using an appropriately Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework where required. This page alone is not a substitute for a working consent mechanism. If advertising or analytics practices change, we will update this notice and any required consent controls accordingly.

Browser-only tools

Our printer problem checker, IPv4 connection lab, paper-fit calculator and troubleshooting notes generator run in the visitor's browser. They do not directly send entered printer addresses, symptoms or notes to the publisher. Do not type passwords, private business network details or personal identifiers into forms or share them publicly. Standard hosting or third-party network requests may still generate technical logs as explained above.

Safety is part of useful guidance.

Examples and diagrams are illustrations unless a real test is explicitly documented. Your device remains under your control.

Data storage and sharing

We keep correspondence only as reasonably needed for the enquiry, legal duties, abuse prevention or dispute resolution. Service providers may retain operational logs and backups according to their policies. Information may be disclosed where legally required or needed to defend a legitimate claim. Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information associated with your enquiry or raise a privacy concern by writing to support@printerresolve.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need reasonable verification, and certain records may need to be retained by law.

Policy updates

If practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Please check this notice periodically.
