Know whether your content fits the page.

A4 and US Letter pages have different proportions. Compare your content dimensions and margins against the selected paper size, then decide what to adjust before wasting paper.

Educational geometric preview only. Printers have device-specific unprintable edges, scaling behavior and borderless media limits that this browser cannot detect.

Enter your page layout

Use millimetres. If you don't know your document's content dimensions, estimate them from your page-layout editor's width and height settings.

Page geometry preview

White: sheet · Dashed: the margins you specified · Blue: content rectangle. Orange means the requested content extends past the margin-defined area. A blue result still may not print edge-to-edge.

What to do with the result

  • It fits: The dimensions fit within your chosen margins geometrically. Check the printer model's hardware margins, paper type and app preview before printing.
  • It doesn't fit: In the document's print settings, use a correct paper size, adjust content size or margins, or use a clearly labeled Fit to page / Shrink oversized pages option where available.
  • A4 vs Letter: A4 is taller and slightly narrower than Letter. A document designed for one may clip or rescale on the other.
  • Borderless printing: Only some devices/media support it; this calculator doesn't verify your printer's capability.
