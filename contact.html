Website and editorial enquiries

Contact Printer Resolve

Looking for printer, Wi-Fi or Windows fixes? Start with our free troubleshooting guides. There is no support request form and no paid remote-support booking on this website.

Email

For editorial corrections, broken links, privacy requests or general website enquiries, write to support@printerresolve.com.

Where we are based

Printer Resolve is published from Kashmir, India. We do not have a physical office in the United States.

Transparency is part of the experience.

Illustrations explain common problems; they are not photographs of devices we have tested. Readers should consult official model guidance for specific procedures.

Report an article mistake or broken reference

To help us check a correction, send the public article URL, the specific step or heading, what you believe is incorrect, and—if available—an official, publicly accessible manufacturer or Microsoft reference. Include a printer model only when relevant, without sharing its serial number. We will assess corrections against the documented model and may update the page; we cannot promise immediate responses or individual repair support.

Protect your information

Never email passwords, one-time passcodes, payment-card numbers, banking details or sensitive personal documents. We do not need remote access to your device to provide access to our free guides.

For warranty repairs, official driver downloads or device-specific assistance, check your printer maker's official support website.
